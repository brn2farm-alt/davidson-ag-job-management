How this app is intended to work
Davidson Ag is your permanent job record and invoice-preparation system. Hnry remains the accounting/invoicing system. Use the Hnry invoice number as the Davidson Ag job number, generate the Hnry-ready lines here, copy them into Hnry, then attach the final Hnry PDF back to this job.
